# TwinkTracker

Build your next level **19, 29 or 39** twink with one compact planning addon for
**WoW Classic Era, Classic Hardcore and Burning Crusade Classic**.

TwinkTracker automatically detects the client and loads the matching Era or TBC
reference. There is no expansion toggle to configure. A discreet footer shows
the active client and addon version, so you always know which data set you are
viewing.

## What you get

- Curated **Tier S, A and B gear** for all nine classes and every relevant slot
- Independent profiles for the **19, 29 and 39 brackets**
- Meaningful faction, role, survival, weapon and random-suffix alternatives
- TBC support for **Blood Elf Paladins** and **Draenei Shamans**
- Class- and bracket-specific **enchants and consumables**
- Clear cautions for item-level gates, professions, binding and unusual
  enchant application routes
- Self-contained skill 1-to-cap preparation guides for **First Aid, Fishing
  and Engineering**, plus **Jewelcrafting in TBC**
- Bracket-specific **Class Tiers**, Twink Basics and automatic visible-map
  Exploration progress
- Normal item tooltips, Shift-click handling and copyable **Wowhead Classic or
  Wowhead TBC links**, selected automatically
- Live equipped-item highlighting, a draggable minimap button and a movable,
  resizable window with per-character settings

TwinkTracker keeps real alternatives together instead of pretending that one
item is always best for every role. Equal choices inside a tier are not ranked
against each other.

## Era and TBC, automatically

The game chooses the correct manifest at login:

- Classic Era / Hardcore loads the Era profiles
- Burning Crusade Classic loads the TBC audits, faction routes, item changes,
  enchants and profession additions

Classic Era and Hardcore retain the **PvP Events** page for confirmed community
battleground sessions, including the level-29 WSG queue every Tuesday at 20:00
server time, and the Era WSG weekend estimate. It is intentionally
hidden in TBC so Era schedules and Firemaw announcements are never presented as
Burning Crusade events.

## Built for safe planning

TwinkTracker is informational only. It does not automate combat, movement,
targeting, equipment, quests, battleground queues or XP gain. It also cannot
lock experience.

Gear and system data have been reviewed against available guides, community
references and expansion-specific Wowhead pages. The result is a curated
planning reference, not a promise of one mathematically proven BiS set. Exact
random suffixes, realm availability, quest state and edge-case enchant rules
may still need confirmation in the live client—especially before spending gold
or committing limited XP.

## Commands

- `/twinktracker`, `/twt` or `/twink` — toggle the addon
- `/tt` — short alias when available
- `/tt show` and `/tt hide` — open or close
- `/tt reset` — reset window position and size
- `/tt help` — show command help

No external addon libraries or required dependencies.
